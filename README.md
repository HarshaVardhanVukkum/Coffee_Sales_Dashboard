#Coffee_Sales_Dashboard

## ☕ Power BI Coffee Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze coffee sales performance across different time periods, stores, product categories, and individual coffee items.

The project transforms raw coffee sales data into meaningful business insights using **Power Query, DAX, data modeling, time intelligence, KPI cards, slicers, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform coffee sales data into an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that helps analyze:

- Overall sales performance
- Monthly and yearly sales trends
- Coffee category performance
- Individual item performance
- Store-level sales performance
- Order and quantity trends
- Gross Sales and Net Sales
- Discount impact on sales
- Year-over-Year sales growth

---

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to dynamically analyze the data based on:

- Year
- Quarter
- Month
- Item Category
- Store Code

Users can combine multiple filters to perform detailed sales analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 📈 Gross Sales by Year Month

A line chart showing the monthly trend of gross sales and helping identify sales growth, decline, and seasonal patterns.

### 📊 Total Sales and Net Sales by Year Month

A combination chart comparing total sales and net sales across different months.

### ☕ Total Sales by Item Category

A bar chart comparing sales performance across different coffee categories.

### 🏆 Total Sales by Item Name

A bar chart displaying the sales contribution of individual coffee products.

### 💸 Discount Amount by Year Month

A waterfall chart showing monthly discount amounts and their overall impact on sales.

### 🎯 KPI Cards

KPI cards provide a high-level summary of:

- Gross Sales
- Net Sales
- Sales YOY
- Sales Last Month
- Total Orders
- Quantity Sold

## 🗂️ Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared using **Power Query** before being loaded into Power BI.

The data preparation process included:

- Importing coffee sales datasets
- Appending multiple datasets
- Data type transformation
- Data cleaning
- Handling missing values
- Creating calculated columns
- Creating date-related fields
- Preparing data for time-based analysis

---

## 🧮 DAX & Calculations

DAX was used to create business measures required for the dashboard.

Some of the major calculations include:

- Gross Sales
- Net Sales
- Total Orders
- Quantity Sold
- Sales YOY
- Sales Last Month
- Discount Amount
- Monthly Sales
- Yearly Sales

Time-intelligence calculations were implemented to support comparison between different time periods.

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 💰 Sales Performance

Analysis of Gross Sales, Net Sales, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, Sales YOY, and Sales Last Month.

### 📅 Time-Based Analysis

Analysis of sales performance across:

- Year
- Quarter
- Month
- Year Month

This helps identify monthly trends and seasonal sales patterns.

### ☕ Category Analysis

Comparison of sales performance across different coffee categories to identify high-performing and low-performing categories.

### 🏆 Item-Level Analysis

Analysis of individual coffee items to identify the best-selling products and their contribution to total sales.

### 🏪 Store Analysis

Store Code filtering allows users to analyze sales performance at individual store levels.

### 💸 Discount Analysis

Monthly discount analysis helps understand how discounts affect overall sales performance and revenue.
